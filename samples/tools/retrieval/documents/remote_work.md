# Remote work

You may work remotely up to three days a week without asking. More than three
days in a week needs your manager's agreement, recorded in the portal so that
it survives a change of manager.

Working from another country needs approval before you travel, because it has
tax and immigration consequences the company has to file for. Ask at least four
weeks ahead. Approval covers a named period and does not roll over.

Equipment for a home office is claimed as an expense, under the same 30-day
rule as any other expense.
