# Onboarding

Your first day is with IT. Collect your laptop, sign in to the corporate
portal, and enrol in two-factor authentication before you request any other
access.

Access to a system is requested through the IT portal and approved by the
system's owner, not by your manager. Approvals usually land within one working
day; chase through the portal rather than by email, because email requests are
not tracked.

Your first-week checklist is in the portal under Onboarding. It is the record
HR reconciles against, so tick items there rather than keeping your own list.
